HOW KIDS EAT

A Sixth Grade Thanksgiving Play

Written and directed by Amanda Young for ACS

A nature documentary comes to the Thanksgiving table. Two narrators with clipboards and safari hats study the North American Sixth Grader and its six kinds of eater: the sensory eater, the kid who eats nothing, the talker, the messy eater, the speed eater, and the overeater.
